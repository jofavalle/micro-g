Known gaps

Partial gravity. None of the 24 investigations was run in partial gravity, such as on the Moon or Mars: 19 are microgravity investigations and 5 are ground investigations. The dashboard does not model what happens between microgravity and normal gravity.

Combustion gases. In the tabular files of PSI that the team explored, BASS-II reports O2, CO2 and CO only at the start and at the end of each test, and Saffire-I, -II and -III report O2 and CO2 over time and no CO. The dashboard publishes no chart of these measurements: the catalog only marks these four investigations with the tag “gas measurements”.
